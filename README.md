# **Nike vs. Adidas Web Analytics Marketing Evaluation**

## **Project Overview**

This project evaluates the web performance and digital marketing strategies of Nike and Adidas using SimilarWeb data.

The analysis compares website traffic, user engagement, geographic traffic, traffic sources, organic and paid search, and social media performance to identify differences between the two brands and opportunities to improve their digital strategies.

## **Business Question**

How do Nike and Adidas compare in website performance and digital marketing effectiveness, and what opportunities exist to improve their online strategies?

The analysis focuses on:

- Website traffic
- User engagement
- Traffic sources
- Geographic traffic
- Organic search
- Paid search
- Social media traffic
- Referral traffic
- Search keywords
- Digital marketing strategy

## **Data Source**

The analysis uses **SimilarWeb** website performance data covering the periods analyzed in the project.

The project compares web analytics metrics for:

- Nike.com
- Adidas.com

## **Web Metrics Analysis**

The analysis evaluates several key website performance metrics, including:

- Total visits
- Average visit duration
- Pages per visit
- Bounce rate
- Traffic by country
- Traffic acquisition channels
- Organic search traffic
- Paid search traffic
- Social media traffic
- Referral traffic

During December 2024–February 2025, Nike recorded 338.3 million website visits compared with 66.54 million for Adidas.

Nike also showed stronger engagement during this period, with an average visit duration of 2 minutes and 58 seconds, 4.68 pages per visit, and a 39.27% bounce rate. Adidas recorded 2 minutes and 26 seconds, 4.36 pages per visit, and a 53.16% bounce rate.

## **Key Findings**

### **Website Traffic & Engagement**

Nike significantly outperformed Adidas in total website traffic.

Nike also demonstrated stronger engagement through longer visit durations, more pages per visit, and a lower bounce rate.

### **Traffic Sources**

Nike demonstrated a more diversified traffic acquisition strategy across direct, organic search, and paid search.

Adidas relied more heavily on direct traffic, suggesting strong brand loyalty but also an opportunity to expand other acquisition channels.

### **Search Analysis**

Nike outperformed Adidas in both organic and paid search traffic.

Nike's search visibility was supported by strong product-related keywords and a broader search strategy.

Adidas showed an opportunity to strengthen SEO, keyword targeting, and paid search campaigns.

### **Social Media Traffic**

Nike generated more traffic through social media channels than Adidas.

The analysis identified opportunities for Adidas to strengthen social media engagement through platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, and influencer partnerships.

### **Geographic Traffic**

Both brands received significant traffic from the United States, United Kingdom, and Japan.

Nike demonstrated broader international reach, while Adidas showed opportunities for growth in North America and other emerging markets.

## Digital Strategy Recommendations

### **Nike**

- Continue refining organic and paid search strategies.
- Expand referral partnerships with sports blogs, fitness platforms, and affiliates.
- Strengthen social commerce through interactive campaigns and influencer collaborations.
- Continue developing personalized and engaging digital content.

### **Adidas**

- Invest in SEO and paid search to increase digital visibility.
- Expand social media marketing and influencer partnerships.
- Strengthen its presence in North America and other growth markets.
- Improve website engagement through personalized shopping experiences.
- Consider tools such as virtual try-ons and faster checkout experiences.

## **Business Insights**

The analysis shows that strong digital performance depends not only on total website traffic but also on how effectively a company attracts and engages visitors across multiple channels.

Nike's stronger combination of traffic volume, engagement, search visibility, and diversified acquisition channels demonstrates a more balanced digital strategy.

Adidas benefits from strong direct traffic and brand loyalty but has opportunities to diversify its acquisition strategy and improve online engagement.
